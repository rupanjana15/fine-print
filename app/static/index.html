Fine Print

A fine-tuned 3B model that reads Terms of Service and flags the clauses that matter, with a severity, a plain-English explanation and the exact quote. Below: ten well-known services, analysed in advance.

Fine Print estimate, not legal advice, and not ToS;DR's grade. How far to trust the grade: on these ten services it matched ToS;DR's human grade for 1 and was within one grade for 5; for 8 of 10 it was more generous. Here it reads one document, while ToS;DR grades the whole service. Reading all documents of 52 companies it never saw, it was exact for 46% and within one grade for 88%; it finds about half as many "blocker" clauses as ToS;DR, so the worst services come out a grade too kind. The individual flags are the more reliable part.

Try your own text: a live "paste your own terms" version is coming. Meanwhile the 4-bit model is public at rupp15/fine-print-gguf and runs on a laptop with llama.cpp at about 1.5–5 s per chunk (prompt and decoding settings on the model card).
